What are deep relaxation sessions?

Deep relaxation sessions combine conversation with a calm, focused state of attention. The approach draws on hypnotherapy, a complementary method in which guided relaxation helps the mind settle and become more receptive to the changes you want to make.

You are awake and aware throughout, and you set the pace. Curious about the exact techniques? I'm glad to explain on your free call, before you decide anything.

Ask a question
What do you mean by deep relaxation sessions?

Deep relaxation sessions combine conversation with guided relaxation drawn from hypnotherapy, a complementary approach in which a calm, focused state helps you work with habits, stress and beliefs. You are awake and aware throughout.

I've never done anything like this. Where do I begin?

Start with the free 15-minute call. No preparation is needed and there are no right answers. You tell me what's on your mind, I explain how sessions work, and we decide together whether this is a good fit.

How do I know if this is right for me?

If stress, sleep, habits, fears or old beliefs feel stuck despite your efforts, that's reason enough to talk. You don't need a diagnosis or a crisis. If you're dealing with severe depression, thoughts of harming yourself, trauma symptoms or a medical condition, please see a doctor or qualified mental health professional first, or alongside. If I think these sessions aren't the right step for you, I'll say so plainly.

What happens in a session?

Sessions last 1 to 1.5 hours. We begin with conversation about what you want to change and what has been getting in the way. Then I guide you through a period of deep relaxation, and we finish by reflecting on what came up and agreeing simple practices for home. Nothing uncomfortable is asked of you.

Will I be aware during a session?

Yes. You'll be deeply relaxed but awake and aware. You can speak, open your eyes or pause at any time, and the pace is always yours.

How many sessions will I need?

Most people come for 6 to 8 sessions, but it varies with what you're working on. After our first conversation, I'll give you an honest view for your situation. You decide when to continue and when to stop.

Can this replace therapy or medication?

No. These sessions are complementary. I don't diagnose or treat medical or mental health conditions, so please continue any treatment your doctor has prescribed, and don't change medication without speaking to them.

Is everything I share confidential?

Yes. What you share stays private. The only exceptions would be where the law requires disclosure, or where someone is at serious risk of harm.

What should I wear or bring?

Come as you are, in comfortable clothes. Nothing needs preparing. Arriving a few minutes early helps you settle.
